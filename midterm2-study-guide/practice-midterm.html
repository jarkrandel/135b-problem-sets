    Problems 2 Practice Midterm

    Here’s a practice midterm to help you prepare. Practice midterm 2 in Janko’s notes is also good preparation.
    🔗

    1.

    Suppose that a Markov chain has the following transition matrix:
    🔗
    \begin{equation*} P = \begin{bmatrix} 0 & 1/2 & 1/2 & 0 & 0\\ 1/3 & 0 & 2/3 & 0 & 0\\ 1/4 & 0 & 0 & 0 & 3/4\\ 0 & 0 & 0 & 1/2 & 1/2\\ 0 & 0 & 0 & 0 & 1 \end{bmatrix}. \end{equation*}
    Determine the classes and identify whether each is recurrent or transient.
    🔗
    🔗

    2.

    Suppose that a branching process has offspring distribution given by
    🔗
    \begin{equation*} p_0 = \frac{1}{5},\quad p_1 = \frac{1}{2},\quad p_2 = \frac{1}{10},\quad p_3 = \frac{1}{5}, \end{equation*}
    and \(p_k = 0\) for all other positive integers \(k\text{.}\)
    🔗
    Hint: It should be helpful to know that \(2s^3+s^2-5s+2 = (s-1)(2s^2+3s-2)\text{.}\)
    🔗
    1. Compute the probability that the process ever dies out.
      🔗
      🔗
    2. Suppose that you know that the process has 30 individuals at generation 22. What is the expected number of individuals at generation 28?
      🔗
      🔗
    🔗

    3.

    Label the four vertices of a square \(1,2,3,4\) in clockwise order. Suppose that an ant transitions between vertices at each time step in one of two ways.
    🔗
    • With probability \(\frac{1}{3}\text{:}\) If the ant is at vertices 1 or 2, it does not move. If it is at vertex 3, it transitions to 4. If it is at vertex 4, it transitions to 3.
      🔗
      🔗
    • With probability \(\frac{2}{3}\text{:}\) The ant moves to the next vertex in clockwise order.
      🔗
      🔗
    1. Write down the transition matrix for the Markov chain whose state at step \(n\) is the vertex that the ant occupies at time \(n\text{.}\)
      🔗
      🔗
    2. Compute \(\lim_{n\rightarrow\infty} P^n_{ij}\text{,}\) for any states \(i\) and \(j\text{.}\) Justify your computation and your usage of any theorems (this doesn’t require a long calculation).
      🔗
      🔗
    3. Compute the expected number of steps it takes the ant to return to state \(4\) once it enters state \(4\text{.}\)
      🔗
      🔗
    🔗

    4.

    Bob made a New Year’s resolution to exercise more regularly. He randomly chooses to exercise or not exercise each day depending on how many times he exercised within the previous two days:
    🔗
    • If Bob exercised 0 times within the previous two days, then he exercises with probability 1.
      🔗
      🔗
    • If Bob exercised \(i\in\{1,2\}\) times within the previous two days, then he exercises with probability \(1-\frac{i}{3}\text{.}\)
      🔗
      🔗
    Consider the Markov chain with states \(1,2,3,4\) labeled \(NN, NE, EN, EE\) respectively where \(X_n = NN\) if Bob did not exercise on days \(n-1\) and \(n\text{,}\) \(X_n = NE\) if Bob did not exercise on day \(n-1\text{,}\) but did exercise on day \(n\text{,}\) etc. Suppose that Bob did not exercise on the two days before New Year’s Day (labeled days \(-1,0\)) so that \(X_0 = 1 = NN\text{.}\)
    🔗
    1. Write out the transition matrix \(P\) for this chain using the labeling of states given above.
      🔗
      🔗
    2. Write an expression for the probability that Bob exercises on days \(3,4,\) and \(6\text{,}\) but not on day 5.
      🔗
      🔗
    3. Write an expression in terms of \(P\) for the expected number of days Bob exercises on days \(8\) and \(9\) combined.
      🔗
      🔗
